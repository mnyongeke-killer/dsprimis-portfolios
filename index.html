About

The person behind DsPrimis

My name is Fritz, but most people know me as DsPrimis. I'm from Haiti, born November 5, 2007, and I picked up coding seriously in 2024 — no computer science degree, no bootcamp. Just a lot of late nights opening an editor, breaking things, and figuring out why they broke until it started making sense.

My work is almost entirely centered on WhatsApp — it's where everyone already spends their time, so it's the platform where a small tool can make the biggest real difference. Bots that search information without leaving the chat, protect a group from spam and unwanted links, or keep a paired number safe. Small improvements to something people already use every day.

Alongside my own projects, I co-founded Syrix, a selective community built to bring developers, innovators and digital creators together under one roof.
